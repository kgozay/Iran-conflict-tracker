# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary users are the owner and a small group of colleagues using the dashboard privately. They need to understand changing conflict-driven market conditions quickly, without learning a complex analytical tool or decoding unnecessary interface detail.

## Product Purpose

JSE Conflict Watch helps users assess how Iran and broader Middle East conflict risk is transmitting into South African markets. Its primary job is to make the current risk regime, important market movements, data quality, and actionable signals understandable at a glance, while supporting deeper investigation when needed.

## Brand Personality

Smooth, editorial, sleek, and professional. The experience should feel composed, informed, and trustworthy under time pressure.

## Anti-references

The product must not resemble a generic dashboard template, an interchangeable AI-generated interface, or a visibly “vibecoded” collection of decorative cards and effects. Visual treatments should be specific to market intelligence and earn their place through clarity.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the decision: make the current regime, its confidence, and its most important drivers immediately clear.
2. Use editorial hierarchy: guide the eye through a deliberate reading order instead of presenting every metric with equal emphasis.
3. Reveal depth progressively: keep the first view calm and scannable while making methodology and detailed data easy to reach.
4. Make trust visible: communicate freshness, provenance, partial coverage, and uncertainty close to the affected information.
5. Prefer purposeful restraint: motion, color, glass effects, and ornament should reinforce state or orientation rather than decorate the interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, sufficient contrast in both themes, non-color status cues, touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels, and layouts that remain usable under zoom and on narrow screens.
